## 1. National Overview: Growth, Scale & Timeline

### Adoption Timeline

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| Feb 2016 | First pickleball clinic held in Cebu (introduced by American Sara Ash) |
| Jun 2017 | First dedicated pickleball venue at LDS Church, Makati |
| 2017 | First pickleball club in the Philippines established |
| Apr 2018 | "Street Pickleball" launched on Emerald Avenue, Ortigas (Pasig) — widely considered the sport's Philippine cultural birthplace |
| Apr 2019 | Philippine Pickleball Sports Association (later renamed PPF) registered with SEC |
| 2020 | Recognized as national sports association; Asian Federation of Pickleball co-founded |
| 2021 | Full IFP member status; only 13 clubs nationally |
| 2022–2023 | Pandemic-driven explosion; players who had converted home driveways/backyards return to courts and bring friends |
| Oct 2023 | Ayala Malls launches "Picklemall" at Market! Market! BGC — first mall pickleball court |
| 2024 | 123+ clubs by August; ~6,500 registered players (PPF); SM opens courts in 30 of 86 malls nationwide including SM City Lanang, Davao |
| May 2024 | Philippine Olympic Committee officially recognizes PPF as National Sports Association |
| Aug 2024 | First Standout Pickleball Championship Invitational (Metro Manila) — MLP format introduced to PH |
| Mar 2025 | PPF at 211 registered clubs, 13,000+ registered members |
| May–Jun 2025 | First Philippine Pickleball National Championship (Marikina City, May 30–June 1, 2025) |
| Aug 2025 | Philippines wins inaugural Pickleball Champions League Asia (Shenzhen) |
| Oct 2025 | PPF at 277 clubs, 18,000 registered members |
| Jan 2026 | National Ranking System officially launched |
| Feb 2026 | PPF launches Philippine Pickleball Participant (PPP) Registry — national player database |
| Mar 2026 | Inaugural Skechers Philippine Pickleball Amateur Nationals (Las Pinas, March 28–30) |

### Current Scale (as of mid-2026)

- **Registered clubs:** 277 (Oct 2025, Wikipedia/PPF)
- **Registered players (PPF):** ~18,000 (Oct 2025)
- **Estimated total active players:** 60,000+ (local organization estimates, 2025; Kolisti Sports)
- **Courts listed by PPF:** 1,070+ (Pickleheads/PPF data)
- **Projected players by 2030:** 200,000+ (Kolisti Sports analysis)
- **National governing body:** Philippine Pickleball Federation (PPF), headquartered in Pasig, Metro Manila. President: Shery Anne Cu; Chairman/Founder: Mike Johnson
- **International affiliation:** Global Pickleball Federation (Class A member with full voting rights); won first Pickleball Champions League Asia title (2025)

**Growth velocity:** From 13 clubs (2021) to 277 clubs (Oct 2025) — roughly 21x in 4 years. Registered players grew from ~6,500 (Aug 2024) to ~18,000 (Oct 2025) — approximately 2.8x in 14 months.

## 2. Key Growth Drivers

### Celebrity & Influencer Adoption
Filipino celebrities playing pickleball have been heavily documented and amplified on social media:
- **Catriona Gray** (Miss Universe 2018) — shared clips from brand events; played with Heart Evangelista
- **Heart Evangelista** — high-profile player/influencer
- **Anne Curtis** — documented player
- **Mimiyuuuh** — popular content creator playing pickleball

The celebrity effect has been especially powerful because Filipino pop culture carries strong aspirational weight, and pickleball is positioned as glamorous, social, and active — all aligned with beauty/wellness influencer aesthetics.

### Mall Infrastructure (Ayala, SM, Robinsons)
All three of the Philippines' major mall groups have added pickleball courts. This is a crucial driver because malls are the primary leisure/social gathering point for Filipino middle and upper-middle classes:
- **Ayala Malls** — first mover (Oct 2023, Market! Market! BGC), launched "Picklemall" concept
- **SM Supermalls** — 30 of 86 Philippine malls have courts by Aug 2024, including SM City Lanang (Davao) and SM CDO Uptown
- **Robinsons Land** — 7+ malls with courts as of July 2024, including Robinsons Tagum (Davao region)

Court rental: typically PHP 200–400/court/session, or free in some SM venues. Shared among 8 players = PHP 50–100/person.

### Pandemic Legacy
The COVID-19 pandemic was the single biggest organic catalyst. Players discovered the sport during lockdowns via YouTube; many built makeshift courts in driveways. When restrictions lifted, they returned to organized play and accelerated club formation.

### Low Barrier to Entry
- Equipment cost: basic paddle PHP 250–1,000 (Shopee/Lazada); carbon-fiber paddle PHP 1,000–2,000+
- Court rental: PHP 50–100/person when shared
- Learning curve: playable within 1–1.5 hours per most accounts
- No costly lessons required to start

### Organizational Maturity
PPF has systematically structured the sport: official national ranking system (Jan 2026), PPP Registry (Feb 2026), national championship (May 2025), national amateur championship (Mar 2026). Major brand sponsorship from Skechers (equipment partner, sponsors top athletes). This organization signals longevity, not fad.

## 3. Player Demographics

### Gender
- No authoritative Philippines-specific gender split exists in public data.
- Global pickleball data shows approximately 43% female share — notably high for a racket sport.
- Qualitative signals from Philippine media: the sport is strongly perceived as women-friendly and community-social; most feature stories profile women prominently.
- Key Philippine women players have won international medals, anchoring female participation as competitive, not just recreational.

**Assessment:** The Philippine women's segment is substantial and growing. The sport's social, low-impact, and intergenerational nature aligns perfectly with how Filipino women socialize (group activity, community bonding). Doubles is the dominant format, further favoring group play.

### Age
- The sport is explicitly cross-generational: documented players range from age 6 to 80+.
- PPF president Armand Tantoco (64) emphasizes inclusivity across age groups.
- Feature profiles in Allure Philippines highlight players aged 17, 22, 30, 31, 50, 52, 54 — all active.
- Senior (50+) women are a notable and growing segment; pickleball is often positioned as an alternative for players whose knees or bodies can no longer handle tennis.

### Socioeconomic Class
- The sport is predominantly AB and upper-C class at present. Indicators:
  - Primary venues are private clubs, mall courts, and dedicated facilities (not free public parks).
  - Equipment at competitive level costs PHP 3,000–10,000+ (paddles) plus shoes, apparel.
  - Players travel to tournaments regionally and internationally (World Pickleball Championship in Bali, Shenzhen, etc.).
  - Top female players (Nicole Eugenio, UP collegiate tennis; Diana Hughes; Regina Posadas, Ateneo de Manila) have professional/educated backgrounds.
- PPF president notes court access is a key barrier; "making do with makeshift courts" is common for those outside the premium venues.
- The sport's stated aspirational goal is to reach C class through barangay/school programs, but that is a medium-term initiative.

### Notable Philippine Women Athletes
- **Nicole Eugenio** (30, Metro Manila) — #1 Philippine female on DUPR, #11 on APP Tour globally; former UP tennis varsity; turned pro 2023; Skechers-sponsored athlete
- **Sarah Jane Lim-Narvasa** (Makati) — #1 Women's Doubles PH, #4 Mixed Doubles PH; 4th at 2023 World Pickleball Championship; Skechers-sponsored athlete
- **Winona "Panda" Cabardo** (22, Davao City) — #1 Women's Singles PH; former tennis player who switched to indoor pickleball after Lupus diagnosis; bronze at 2023 World Pickleball Championship; directly relevant as a **Davao-based icon**
- **Diana Hughes** (42) — "Wonder Woman of Philippine Pickleball"; one of the most decorated Filipina pickleball athletes (Inquirer profile)

## 5. Apparel & Paddle Market: What Players Buy & Where

### Paddles

**Entry-level (casual/beginner):**
- PHP 250–1,000 on Shopee and Lazada; wooden or basic composite
- Decathlon PH sells entry-level paddles in-store and online (Decathlon.ph); also listed on Lazada

**Mid-range to performance:**
- PHP 1,000–5,000 on Shopee, Lazada, and specialty stores
- Carbon fiber paddles (USAPA-approved, 3K carbon fiber) widely available online
- Selkirk, Joola, CRBN, Paddletek available through PickleballPH.com

**Premium/tournament-grade:**
- PHP 5,000–20,000+; mostly through specialist stores
- **PickleballPH** (pickleballph.com) — the primary dedicated online store; 4 physical "Stride & Stroke" retail stores: Ayala Malls Manila Bay, Alabang Town Center, Ayala Center Cebu, Glorietta 4 (all in NCR/Cebu; no Davao store confirmed). Ships nationwide; free shipping; Billease installments accepted. Carries Joola, CRBN, Paddletek, Selkirk brands.

### Apparel

**The current PH market for pickleball-specific apparel is thin and heavily skewed toward imported/Western brands.**

**What PickleballPH.com currently sells (apparel):**
- 21 products total — predominantly men's items (shirts, shorts); very limited women's items
- **Only 1 dedicated women's bottom confirmed: Paddletek Performance Women's Skort at PHP 4,250**
- Paddletek Performance 7" Women's Short: PHP 3,650
- Men's shirts: PHP 2,475–3,450; Men's shorts: PHP 2,750–4,350
- Hats/visors: PHP 1,500–2,360
- All brands are American (Joola, CRBN, Paddletek) — imported

**Secondary market / general activewear:**
- Carousell Philippines lists secondhand pickleball/tennis skirts; international brands (Yonex, Nike, Lululemon, Fila, Alala)
- Zalora.com.ph ran a feature "7 Days, 7 Pickleball Looks" (2025) — using general athletic/tennis wear styled for pickleball; no dedicated local pickleball women's apparel brand mentioned
- Women currently wear general athletic wear (leggings, sports bras, tennis-style skorts) from Decathlon, H&M Sport, Uniqlo, or premium international brands ordered from US or via Carousell resellers

**The gap is explicit:** There is no dedicated premium women's pickleball apparel brand based in the Philippines. PickleballPH's apparel section is sparse and male-focused. Local women are wearing generic activewear, international tennis brands (not sized for Asian/Filipina bodies), or expensive imported US pickleball brands.

**Court rental pricing context:** PHP 200–400/court (shared = PHP 50–100/person); indoor (PickleTown Davao) likely at the higher end.

## 6. Trends, Seasonality & Community Culture

### Social & Cultural Dynamics
- **Doubles is the dominant format** in the Philippines — it's fundamentally a social, group sport here
- **Community bonds are primary motivation** — players mention friendships, post-pandemic social connection, family bonding as key draws
- **"Kumare" / "suki" culture** — Filipinos build strong in-group loyalty; once a player joins a club or plays regularly at a venue, they become regulars and evangelists
- **Intergenerational play** — parents, grandparents, and children on the same court; a family activity
- **Women's group dynamics** — women's doubles groups often form via friendship networks and community (village/gym/workplace); they share courts and invest together (equipment, apparel, club fees)
- **Tournament culture is growing fast** — from zero PPF-sanctioned tournaments to a national championship in 5 years; players are highly motivated by competition brackets and medals

### Aspirational Styling
- Allure Philippines' June 2025 pickleball feature explicitly uses lifestyle/beauty-magazine framing: players describe what they wear, the social dimension, and looking good on court
- The Zalora blog "7 Days, 7 Pickleball Looks" signals mainstream fashion interest in pickleball aesthetics
- Players from upper-AB class reference Lululemon-style aspiration; heart of the market wants to look premium but many are underserved by sizing (US brands do not fit "Asian fit" bodies well)
- Skechers' partnership with PPF and development of dedicated pickleball footwear is the first major brand making pickleball-specific products for the Philippine market — apparel is the next frontier

### Seasonality
- **No explicit seasonal pattern identified** in Philippines data; the sport is year-round
- Philippines has no winter, so weather is the constraint, not cold seasons
- **Rain disrupts outdoor play** (documented at MTS 25th Anniversary Tournament — rain delay); indoor venues like PickleTown Davao (10 courts, 7AM–12MN) are premium partly because they are weather-proof
- **Weekend peak demand:** consistently reported across all sources; court availability is the #1 constraint

### Pricing Sensitivity
- Players at the competitive level willing to pay PHP 3,500–10,000+ for paddles
- Women's skorts: only data point is PHP 4,250 (Paddletek via PickleballPH) — imported, limited stock
- General activewear (Decathlon): PHP 500–1,500 range for shorts/tights
- Market shows appetite for premium product if quality/fit is right, but also notes scam risk (online paddle buyers being defrauded) — trust is important

## 7. Implications for B Active

### Opportunity Validation
The research strongly validates B Active's market hypothesis:
1. Pickleball in the Philippines is real, fast-growing, and structurally sound (federation, national rankings, Olympic recognition, major brand sponsors)
2. Davao City specifically has an active, tournament-playing, community-organized pickleball scene
3. The women's segment is prominent, aspirational, and **completely underserved by dedicated premium women's pickleball apparel**
4. The one women's skort available via the main PH pickleball retailer is an imported US brand at PHP 4,250 — proving price ceiling acceptance exists and showing the product type is desired

### Specific Actionable Recommendations

**1. Own the "Davao-first, PH-proud" premium women's pickleball identity**
No other brand is doing this. B Active can be the first premium Filipino women's pickleball apparel brand, based in Davao — the sport's rising Mindanao hub. This is a genuine first-mover advantage. Lead with "proudly Davao" messaging in local marketing, then expand nationally.

**2. Design for Asian fit from day one**
US brands (Paddletek, Joola, Lucky in Love) are not sized or proportioned for Filipina bodies. "Asian fit" is the brand's stated direction — make this explicit in product descriptions, sizing guides, and marketing. This is a concrete pain point for women currently importing or ordering from abroad.

**3. Target the core demographic precisely: women 28–50, AB/upper-C class, urban Davao**
Winona Cabardo is the aspirational athletic ideal (young, Davao-based, world-ranked); Regina Posadas (54, sponsored athlete, active and stylish) is the inspirational lifestyle ideal. Both archetypes need to be in B Active's story. The doubles pairs of women playing 3x per week are the core buyer: they want to look good together on court.

**4. Prioritize skorts and dresses — the category gap is loudest here**
PickleballPH's apparel section has no women's dress and one skort (imported, limited sizes). Skorts and athletic dresses are the definitive women's pickleball silhouette globally. B Active's catalog (skorts, sets, dresses) directly addresses the gap. Price range PHP 1,500–3,500 for skorts/dresses would undercut the imported PHP 4,250 option while feeling premium versus Decathlon.

**5. Build a Davao retail/court presence before going national online**
PickleballPH has no Davao physical store. Partner with PickleTown Davao (10-court indoor facility, 7AM–12MN) as a retail/display point or become the official apparel partner. Sponsor one MTS tournament or the DCPCI invitational — tournament exposure to the exact target customer is highly efficient.

**6. Engage Winona Cabardo and local coaches as brand ambassadors**
Winona Cabardo is Davao's most famous pickleball player nationally — she is a local hero with international credentials and a compelling personal story. Even a social media partnership would be highly credible. Coach Kevin Ken Elechichon (Selkirk advocate, MTS Batang Pickleball Academy) is a key community influencer for Davao.

**7. Build on Shopee/Lazada for national reach, but drive Davao loyalty first**
Most pickleball equipment in PH is bought via Shopee and Lazada. B Active should have a Shopee/Lazada presence for discoverability, but the brand story, premium feel, and community connection are built through the bactiveph.com direct channel and through court/club engagement.

**8. Content strategy: style-forward women's pickleball content in Tagalog/Bisaya**
There is zero dedicated women's pickleball fashion/style content in the Philippines market. B Active can own this content niche: court looks, styling tips, player spotlights, community stories. Allure Philippines' pickleball feature (Jun 2025) proves lifestyle media will cover this topic — B Active should be the brand these outlets call for comment.

**9. Price positioning: PHP 1,200–3,500 for apparel is the right range**
- Below the imported Paddletek skort (PHP 4,250) to remove sticker shock
- Above Decathlon (PHP 500–1,500) to signal premium
- Comparable to Filipina women's willingness-to-pay signals in adjacent premium activewear markets
- Sets (top + bottom) can command PHP 3,000–5,500 as a bundle

**10. Watch the Batang Pickleball Academy (MTS) — youth program signal**
Youth programs (Selkirk/MTS partnership) mean the next wave of women's players is being formed now. B Active's 5-year opportunity includes being the brand that those 10–16 year olds see their "titas" wearing when they start playing.
