## 1. Market Size & Growth

### Philippine Activewear & Athleisure

- **Philippines activewear market:** USD 1.86 billion in 2024, projected to reach USD 3.60 billion by 2033 — **CAGR ~6.8%** (IMARC Group, 2025).
- **Broader sportswear market:** USD 3.96 billion in 2024, reaching USD 5.51 billion by 2033 at ~3.7% CAGR (IMARC Group, 2025).
- **Philippines athleisure market (Ken Research):** Tracked from 2019–2030 with consistent growth driven by gym memberships, wellness culture, and remote/hybrid work casualization.
- **Women's segment:** Fastest-growing sub-segment driven by rising female participation in fitness, yoga, and court sports; no single national figure for women-only share, but the global women's activewear market is expanding at a higher CAGR than men's (Allied Market Research, 2025).

### Philippine E-Commerce (All Categories)

- **Total PH e-commerce market:** USD 28 billion in 2025, projected USD 86.2 billion by 2034 — **CAGR 13.3%** (IMARC, 2025).
- **Top 3 marketplace GMV (Shopee + Lazada + TikTok Shop):** USD 22 billion in FY 2025, up 15% from USD 19 billion in FY 2024 (Cube Asia Tradewinds, April 2026).
- **Fashion & apparel:** #1 category on Shopee (27% of GMV) and #2 on TikTok Shop (26%); this makes apparel the single most important category in PH e-commerce.

### Pickleball Market (Philippines)

- Active players estimated at **60,000+** as of 2025; projected to surpass **200,000 by 2030** (Kolisti Sports research, October 2025).
- Courts being established across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao — Davao is specifically cited as an active and growing market.
- Global pickleball clothing & apparel market: USD 250 million in 2024, forecast USD 1.2 billion by 2033 at **CAGR ~18.8%** (Verified Market Reports, 2025).

**Confidence level:** High for top-line figures. Women's-specific activewear split is estimated/inferred.

## 2. Online Shopping Behavior & Platform Landscape

### Platform Market Share (FY 2025, Cube Asia Tradewinds — April 2026)

| Platform | GMV (FY 2025) | YoY Growth | Market Share | Avg. Order Value |
|----------|--------------|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Shopee | USD 12 billion | +25% | 55% | PHP 316 |
| TikTok Shop | USD 6 billion | +53% | 29% | PHP 198 |
| Lazada | USD 3 billion | -34% | 16% | PHP 416 |

**Key takeaways:**
- Shopee is dominant and growing; Filipino shoppers placed an average **11 Shopee orders per year** in 2025.
- TikTok Shop is the fastest-growing and now the **second-largest** platform by Q4 2025. Its low AOV (PHP 198) reflects impulse/discovery buying.
- Lazada is contracting (-34%) but retains the highest AOV (PHP 416), increasingly specializing in premium/branded goods.
- **Shopee notified PH sellers in early 2026 that J&T Express can no longer be selected as a logistics partner**, consolidating fulfillment through Shopee's own delivery network.

### What Drives Purchases on Each Platform

- **Shopee:** Campaign-driven (9.9, 11.11, 12.12, etc.); repeat purchasing; free shipping vouchers; flash sales. Q4 alone = 31% of Shopee's annual PH GMV.
- **TikTok Shop:** Discovery via algorithmic video feed and live-selling; impulse-driven; Beauty & personal care (#1 at 28% of GMV), Fashion & apparel (#2 at 26%).
- **Lazada:** LazMall brand stores; authenticity guarantees; higher-consideration purchases. Electronics & appliances = 26% of Lazada GMV.

### Social Commerce & Live Selling

- The Philippines has **one of the highest live-selling adoption rates in Southeast Asia** (Cube Asia, 2026).
- TikTok Shop's beauty dominance (28% GMV share) is direct evidence of live-selling converting viewers to buyers.
- Facebook Live and Instagram Live are also active commerce channels — particularly for smaller brands without a Shopee/Lazada footprint.
- Filipino consumers are "highly motivated by promotions like 'Buy 1, Take 1,' free shipping, and limited-time discounts" (LOKAL/LKL.ai, 2025).

### DTC Website vs. Marketplace for a Premium Brand

- **Both are necessary**, but the roles differ:
  - **Marketplace (Shopee/TikTok Shop):** Volume, discovery, and reach — essential for early awareness.
  - **DTC Website:** Brand control, storytelling, full margin retention, and customer data ownership — essential for premium positioning.
- A notable Philippines precedent: PLDT launched on marketplaces first for traffic volume, then migrated loyal customers to its own DTC portal (Truelogic, 2025).
- Lazada Philippines commission rates in 2025: 1%–5% for standard sellers, ~11% for LazMall — plus payment and campaign fees that compound margin pressure (Oliver Revelo, 2026).
- **For a premium brand like B Active:** The DTC website is the brand's "true home" and should be treated as the primary brand experience. Marketplaces are acquisition channels, not brand-building channels.

## 3. Payments

### Digital Payments Overview (2024–2025)

- Digital payments accounted for **57.4% of retail transaction volume** and **59% of transaction value** in 2024, up from 52.8%/55.3% in 2023 (BSP data via KoinGeek, 2025).
- Mobile wallet users: **75.5 million projected in 2025** (from 24.6 million in 2020) — one of the steepest digital wallet growth curves globally (Thunes, 2025).

### GCash

- **~89% mobile wallet market share** (Statista, 2023 — most recent available).
- **94 million registered users** as of 2025 (PayMongo/webdesigner.ph).
- **41% of e-commerce transaction value** goes through digital wallets, primarily GCash (Antom, 2025).
- QR code payments (primarily GCash) account for ~60% of all digital transactions in the Philippines as of early 2026.
- GCash dominates retail adoption at **85% share among sari-sari store owners** surveyed in 2025 (Rappler, 2025).
- **For any PH e-commerce brand: GCash acceptance is non-negotiable.**

### Maya (formerly PayMaya)

- Second-largest wallet at ~11–15% share; important for completeness.
- GCash + Maya together cover ~90% of Philippine digital wallet users.

### Cash on Delivery (COD)

- **COD is still preferred by ~68% of Filipino online shoppers** (RichestPH, 2025).
- Especially dominant outside Metro Manila (provincial customers).
- **Critical risk — Return to Sender (RTS/RTO):** Industry estimates suggest **20–40% of COD orders in PH result in RTO** (Ninja Van blog; Cloud Ecommerce, 2026). Inaccurate product expectations drive 40% of COD returns.
- COD RTO creates double shipping costs (outbound + return) and inventory delays.
- Sellers bear both the outbound and return shipping fee when an order is refused.

### Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL)

- **BillEase** and **Atome** are the leading BNPL providers in the Philippines.
- Also: **SPayLater** (Shopee's native BNPL) — reached mid-teens adoption on Shopee in 2025.
- GrabPay Later also active.
- BNPL is growing especially among Millennials and Gen Z; allows customers to afford higher-ticket items.
- **Particularly relevant for B Active** given price points (dresses ₱895–2,450): BNPL reduces cart abandonment.

### Credit/Debit Cards

- Visa and Mastercard remain standard offerings; JCB common in PH.
- Cards important for higher-value purchases and for corporate/business buyers.

### Bank Transfer (InstaPay / PESONet)

- InstaPay and PESONet (online banking) widely offered through payment gateways.
- BPI and UnionBank direct debit are popular premium options.

### What Payment Methods a PH Store MUST Offer

| Tier | Method | Priority |
|------|---------|----------|
| Essential | GCash | Must-have |
| Essential | COD (Cash on Delivery) | Must-have (esp. for provincial reach) |
| Essential | Credit/Debit Card (Visa/MC) | Must-have |
| Strongly Recommended | Maya | High |
| Strongly Recommended | BillEase or Atome (BNPL) | High |
| Recommended | GrabPay | Medium |
| Recommended | InstaPay/Online Banking | Medium |

## 4. Logistics & Shipping

### Major National Couriers (Philippines, 2026)

| Courier | Metro Manila Rate (≤1kg) | Provincial Rate (Luzon) | Mindanao Rate | On-Time (Dec 2025) | COD Fee | COD Remittance |
|---------|------------------------|------------------------|---------------|-------------------|---------|---------------|
| J&T Express | ₱65 (bulk ₱52) | ₱85 | ₱105 | 78% | 2.5% | Tue & Fri |
| Ninja Van | ₱70 (bulk ₱55) | ₱90 | ₱110 | 81% | 2.8% | Twice weekly |
| Flash Express | ₱60 (bulk ₱48) | ₱80 | ₱100 | 72% | 2.3% | Weekly |
| LBC | From ₱39 (local) | Varies | Varies | N/A | Available | Varies |

*Source: Cloud Ecommerce, March 2026. Rates are base estimates; actual rates depend on contract volume and weight.*

**Additional context:**
- **J&T Express:** Market leader (2M+ parcels/day); covers all 81 provinces; 1,200+ service points. **Note: Shopee PH has removed J&T as a selectable courier for sellers on its platform (early 2026)** — this affects sellers whose primary channel is Shopee but doesn't affect standalone WooCommerce stores.
- **Ninja Van:** Best for Metro Manila premium service; 800+ pickup points; best technology/tracking; highest first-call resolution (78%). Good choice for premium brand shipments.
- **Flash Express:** Cost-effective; 600+ centers; strong in urban areas; provincial coverage still expanding (68 provinces as of March 2026). Weakest on-time delivery during peak.
- **LBC Express:** Legacy brand with 80 years in PH; extensive provincial network; good for remote areas.

### Davao City — Local Delivery

- **Lalamove:** Available in Davao; Philippines' #1 express delivery for on-demand/same-day; motorcycle and vehicle options.
- **GrabExpress:** Available in Davao; on-demand parcel delivery with live GPS tracking; budget-friendly.
- **Borzo (formerly MrSpeedy):** Also operates in Davao; known for affordable rates.
- J&T, Ninja Van, and Flash Express all serve Davao for standard shipping.

### Delivery Times (Standard)

- Metro Manila to Cebu: LBC 2–3 days; J&T 2–4 days; Ninja Van 3–5 days.
- Davao to Metro Manila: Expect 3–5 business days via major couriers.
- Same-day delivery is available within Davao City via Lalamove/GrabExpress.

### Free Shipping Thresholds

- **Philippine e-commerce norm:** Most brands set free shipping thresholds at psychologically appealing numbers like **₱1,000**.
- Shopee and Lazada run frequent free shipping promotions that have trained Filipino buyers to expect free shipping.
- **Optimal threshold formula:** 15–30% above current average order value (Cloud Ecommerce, 2026).
- Offering blanket free shipping everywhere is economically risky for a Davao-based DTC store — provincial delivery costs are higher.
- Strategy: Free shipping to Davao City/Region XI unconditionally; free nationwide shipping above a threshold (e.g., ₱1,499–₱1,999).

### Fulfillment Considerations for Davao-Based Store

- Davao is well-served by all major couriers for outbound nationwide shipping.
- Same-day delivery for local Davao customers is viable via Lalamove/GrabExpress — a competitive advantage.
- COD remittance from provincial deliveries can take 1–2 weeks; factor into cash flow planning.
- Peak season (November–December) consistently strains courier capacity nationwide — plan inventory and shipment lead times accordingly.

## 5. Trust Signals & Returns

### What Filipino Online Shoppers Expect Before Buying

1. **Cash on Delivery option** — even if they end up paying digitally, the existence of COD signals legitimacy to many Filipino buyers.
2. **Customer reviews and ratings** — among the top 3 purchase motivators; negative or absent reviews kill conversion.
3. **Responsive Facebook/Instagram messaging** — Filipino consumers expect fast replies (within hours); many will message a brand before ordering to verify it's real.
4. **Authentic-looking social media presence** — active posting, real product photos, real customer tags.
5. **Clear returns/exchange policy** — consumers who can't easily return won't buy, especially for apparel where fit is uncertain.
6. **Business legitimacy signals** — SEC/DTI registration badge, physical address (the Davao store is a strong trust signal), business registration number.
7. **Secure payment logos** — GCash, Visa/MC, BillEase logos on checkout reassure buyers.
8. **Free shipping** or clearly stated shipping fee — surprise shipping costs at checkout are a primary cart abandonment cause.

### Returns Norms in the Philippines

- E-commerce returns are growing but the culture of returns is less developed than in Western markets.
- **COD refusal at delivery is the most common "return"** — buyer simply declines the package.
- Formal return/exchange requests are less common but expected to be simple and fast.
- Republic Act 7394 (Consumer Act) provides basic consumer protection; DTI has strengthened online purchase return standards.
- Industry norm: 7–15 day exchange/return window for apparel; defective items returnable.
- **Implication:** A clear, friendly returns policy (even if strict) builds trust more than it increases returns volume.

### Online Scam Context

- 10,000 reported scam cases in 2024; 38% involved online shopping and delivery scams (CICC data, 2025).
- This is why trust signals matter so much — Filipino buyers have learned to be cautious with unknown brands.
- A standalone DTC site without trust signals will struggle; marketplaces provide built-in trust infrastructure.

## 7. WooCommerce Technical Stack

### Recommended Payment Gateways

#### PayMongo (Primary Recommendation)

- **Philippine-built and BSP-licensed** payment gateway.
- Official WooCommerce plugin: `wc-paymongo-payment-gateway` (WordPress.org); updated April 17, 2026; tested compatible with WC 10.7.0 and WP 6.9.4.
- **Accepts:** Visa, Mastercard, GCash, GrabPay, Maya, Atome (BNPL), BPI (online banking), UnionBank, BillEase.
- **Fees:** 3.5% + ₱15 per credit card transaction; 3% for e-wallets.
- **Minimum transaction:** ₱100.
- **Currency:** PHP only (correct for B Active).
- Developer docs: developers.paymongo.com/docs/woocommerce
- **One plugin covers GCash + Maya + cards + BNPL** — ideal for a small team.

#### Xendit (Alternative/Supplement)

- Competitive fees: 2.9% + ₱15 for cards; 2.5% for e-wallets; 1.5% + ₱20 for direct debit.
- WooCommerce plugin available via WordPress dashboard.
- More complex to set up; better for higher-volume operations or if PayMongo rates become a concern.
- Offers volume discounts.

#### Dragonpay

- Specialized in over-the-counter (OTC) payments and online banking.
- Useful for customers without digital wallets or cards (7-Eleven, Cebuana Lhuillier, etc.).
- Limited WooCommerce plugin support; integration is more manual.
- **Recommended only as a secondary option** if B Active wants maximum payment inclusivity for non-digital customers.

### COD on WooCommerce

- WooCommerce has native COD payment method (no plugin needed).
- Coordinate COD collection/remittance directly with chosen courier(s) (J&T, Ninja Van, or Flash Express all support COD).

### Shipping Plugins for WooCommerce

- **WooCommerce Shipping (native):** Handles flat-rate and free shipping rules; sufficient for initial setup.
- **Shipmates (shipmates.app):** Philippines-specific multi-courier plugin; integrates with J&T Express, Ninja Van, Flash Express, LBC, and others; generates automated waybills; tracks shipments. Strongly recommended for operational efficiency.
- **Lalamove Business API:** Can be integrated for same-day local Davao delivery; requires developer setup or third-party connector.
- **Ninja Van WooCommerce Plugin:** Official Ninja Van plugin available for label generation and tracking.

## 8. Implications for B Active

### 8.1 Channel Strategy

**Recommended Approach: DTC-first with Marketplace expansion**

| Phase | Channel | Priority |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Phase 1 (Launch) | WooCommerce DTC site (bactiveph.com) | Primary — brand home, full margin, customer ownership |
| Phase 1 (Launch) | Shopee shop | Secondary — reach, discovery, campaign traffic |
| Phase 2 (3–6 months) | TikTok Shop | High priority — live-selling for fashion/activewear; 53% growth in PH |
| Phase 3 (6–12 months) | Instagram/Facebook shops | Complementary social commerce |
| Later/Optional | Lazada | Low priority — declining share; consider only if premium brand positioning fits LazMall |

**Rationale:** The DTC website builds brand equity and customer database. Shopee captures impulse buyers. TikTok Shop is the fastest-growing channel for fashion and is tailor-made for live demonstration of activewear.

### 8.2 Payment Methods — Must-Have List

1. **GCash** (via PayMongo plugin) — non-negotiable; 89% wallet market share
2. **COD** (WooCommerce native + courier agreement) — non-negotiable for provincial reach
3. **Visa/Mastercard** (via PayMongo) — non-negotiable
4. **Maya** (via PayMongo) — strongly recommended
5. **BillEase or Atome BNPL** (via PayMongo/Atome plugin) — strongly recommended given price points ₱895–2,450

**Recommended implementation:** Start with PayMongo (covers GCash + Maya + cards + BNPL in one plugin) + WooCommerce native COD. Add Dragonpay OTC later only if there's demand from non-digital-wallet customers.

### 8.3 COD Risk Management

- **COD RTO is a serious business risk** (20–40% of COD orders nationally). Strategies to reduce:
  - Order confirmation SMS/Viber/WhatsApp before shipping
  - Prepayment incentive (small discount for GCash/card payment)
  - Clear product photos and sizing guides to reduce expectation mismatch
  - Limit COD to orders under ₱2,000 (reduces exposure on high-value items)
  - Require contact number verification before processing COD orders

### 8.4 Primary Courier Recommendation

**For B Active (Davao-based, premium brand, nationwide shipping):**

- **Primary national courier: Ninja Van** — best on-time reliability (81%), best tracking/tech, best merchant support, good Mindanao coverage. Aligns with a premium brand that can't afford lost/delayed packages.
- **Backup national courier: J&T Express** — widest provincial coverage (all 81 provinces); useful for remote areas Ninja Van may not serve as well.
- **Local Davao same-day: Lalamove** — for Davao customers who want immediate delivery from the physical store.
- **Avoid Flash Express as primary** — lowest on-time delivery rate (72% in December 2025); higher complaint rate.

### 8.5 Free Shipping Threshold

- **Recommended threshold: ₱1,499 free shipping nationwide.**
- Rationale: B Active's average order value is likely ₱1,200–₱1,800 (single dress or skort + top). ₱1,499 is achievable with a small add-on, drives AOV up, and is psychologically clean.
- **Davao City (local): free shipping unconditionally** — local customers near the store are the easiest to convert and retain.
- Consider promoting "FREE shipping on orders ₱1,499+" as a hero trust signal on the homepage.

### 8.6 Trust Signal Checklist for bactiveph.com

- [ ] Prominent GCash, Visa/MC, BillEase payment logos at checkout and footer
- [ ] COD available (stated clearly on product pages)
- [ ] Physical store address (Davao) displayed in footer and About page
- [ ] Facebook/Instagram links with active presence; Messenger chat widget on site
- [ ] Customer reviews on product pages (import from Facebook, Google, or use a WooCommerce review plugin)
- [ ] Clear size guide for all apparel
- [ ] Returns/exchange policy: 7-day exchange for unworn items; defective items returnable
- [ ] Business registration number (DTI/SEC)
- [ ] HTTPS and security trust badge

### 8.7 Live Selling Opportunity

- **TikTok Shop live selling is the #1 untapped channel for premium Philippine fashion brands.**
- Fashion is #2 on TikTok Shop (26% of GMV); live-selling converts at higher rates than static listings.
- B Active's pickleball dresses and skorts are visual, demonstrable products — ideal for live-sell format.
- Recommended: 1–2 TikTok live sessions per week, timed around post-work hours (7–9 PM); partner with a local Davao pickleball ambassador/KOL for first sessions.

### 8.8 Q4 Priority

- Q4 (October–December) = 31% of Shopee's annual PH GMV. Begin inventory build in August/September.
- 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12 are all major campaign events. B Active should plan promotions for each.
- Consider a "Holiday Play" gift set bundle (dress + paddle) timed for 11.11 and 12.12.
