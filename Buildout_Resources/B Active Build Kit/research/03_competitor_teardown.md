## PART B — PICKLEBALL-SPECIFIC APPAREL: CONVENTIONS & PDP STANDARDS

### Key Brands Researched
Based on fetches and searches: Stack Athletics, Lucky in Love, Pickleball Bella, Jofit, Pillar Athletics, Lululemon (tennis/pickleball), Alo (tennis-inspired), Athleta (Court + Course edit), Beyond Yoga (Course + Court Collection), Halara (active dress), PickleballPlaywear.com, GearPickle.com.

### Standard PDP Features for Premium Pickleball Dresses

**1. Built-In Compression / Undergarment**
- Detachable undershorts (Stack Athletics standard: 3" inseam, Slip-free)
- Integrated compression shorts with side pockets
- "Romper-style" inner (all-in-one, no separation)
- Built-in shelf bra (Lululemon) or full sports bra support labeled by support level (Light/Medium/High)
- Removable bra pads called out explicitly

**2. Ball Pockets**
- Dedicated ball-sized pocket(s) in inner shorts or skirt lining
- Separate phone pocket (usually side, with zipper)
- Pocket "tested by jogging and sprinting to ensure balls do not pop out"
- Two-pocket standard: one ball, one phone

**3. Fabric Copy Conventions**
- Moisture-wicking with % callout (e.g., "76% Nylon, 24% Spandex")
- UPF rating (UPF 50+ is a standard premium claim for outdoor court sports)
- 4-way stretch explicit mention
- Lightweight with grams-per-meter if possible
- Breathable mesh panels in back or sides

**4. Length & Fit Callouts**
- Total dress length in inches (measured at size Small)
- Inseam length of undershort
- Fit type: Slim / Relaxed / Flowing
- Model height and size worn stated explicitly

**5. Photography Standards (Premium Pickleball)**
- On-court action shots (mid-swing or mid-serve)
- Close-up pocket demonstration
- Undershort/construction detail shots
- Color swatches named evocatively, not generically
- 5–15 images per colorway including lifestyle, detail, and flat-lay

**6. 2025–2026 Design Trends**
- Bubble hem silhouettes as dominant trend
- Sage green, butter yellow, coral, dusty rose dominant colors
- "Court-to-cafe" positioning (wearable off-court)
- High-waisted fits pairing with crop tops
- Pleated skirt panel over compression short (skort hybrid)

### Positioning Language Conventions
- "From warm-ups to winning shots" (Stack)
- "Court-ready look" (Lululemon)
- "Court to café" (Alo)
- "Elevated on and off the court" (Pillar)
- B Active equivalent should reference the Davao/Philippine court context — "From the courts of Davao to the streets of the city"

## PART C — AVOID: Fast-Fashion & Mass-Market Anti-Patterns

### 1. Shein (us.shein.com)

**Fetch status:** Full HTML fetched successfully.

#### Documented Dark Patterns
- 18 dark patterns identified by Rouge Media study — the highest of any retailer studied
- Countdown timers on items ("Flash Sale: 2:47:33 remaining")
- "X people viewing this right now" false scarcity
- Multiple animated pop-up windows on landing
- Banner ads intermixed with product grid — distinction between content and advertising deliberately blurred
- Navigation: 26 top-level categories including Cell Phones, Automotive, Pet Supplies — fashion buried in a marketplace
- Product images: inconsistent quality, mix of flat lays and low-light studio shots
- Price anchoring: original prices shown in strikethrough with no credible original retail basis ("$7.76" with "34% OFF")
- Homepage is an infinite scroll of promotional banners, not editorial

#### Anti-Pattern Signals for B Active to Avoid
- Countdown timers on any product or cart page
- "X people viewing" urgency notifications
- Pop-up overlays within first 3 seconds of landing
- Mixing product categories to appear larger (Shein sells toys alongside activewear)
- Discount badges on every product card in grid view

### Site Architecture & Navigation

| DO | DON'T |

| DO | DON'T |
|---|---|
| Consistent lighting, soft natural tones, Philippine outdoor settings | Studio-only white backgrounds (signals catalog, not lifestyle) |
| Asian-fit models at relevant body types for the PH market | Exclusively Western/international models |
| On-court action shots AND lifestyle shots (café, promenade) | Only static stand-and-smile shots |
| 5–10 product images per colorway including detail/pocket shots | 1–2 images per product |

| DO | DON'T |
|---|---|

| DO | DON'T |
|---|---|

| DO | DON'T |

| Clear Philippine-based brand story (Davao, Asian fit, local courts) | Generic "premium activewear" copy with no origin |

### Technical / UX
| DO | DON'T |
|---|---|
| Email capture popup with 5-second delay (first visit only) | Immediate popup on page load |

## Implications for B Active

### Strategic Priority 1 — Own Pickleball in the Philippines
No other brand is owning pickleball apparel with premium positioning in the Philippines. The market is wide open. B Active should use explicit pickleball-first navigation, copy, and photography before expanding to broader activewear language.

### Strategic Priority 2 — Asian Fit as a Brand Pillar
Both Lululemon and Alo use Western-default sizing. B Active's "Asian fit" positioning is a genuine competitive advantage — not a caveat. Call it out as a feature, not an apology. Every size guide and PDP should reference Asian fit context.

### Strategic Priority 3 — Premium Technical Language on a DTC Budget
Lululemon and Alo spend millions on photography. B Active can match the *vocabulary* of premium for near-zero cost: branded fabric names, feature bullet lists, UPF callouts, inseam measurements. These are writing and product development decisions, not photography budget.

### Strategic Priority 4 — Trust Architecture for a New Brand
B Active is unknown. Every page must work harder to build trust: founder story visible on homepage, quality guarantee as a content block, real PH model photography, clear returns policy, GCash/COD payment options. Do not let WooCommerce default settings make the checkout feel generic.

### Strategic Priority 5 — Avoid the Discount Trap from Day One
The biggest risk is launching with a sale banner. Once customers expect discounts, premium pricing is impossible to reclaim. Stack Athletics (a pickleball-specific brand) runs sitewide 50% sales that undermine its own premium claims. B Active must resist this from launch.
