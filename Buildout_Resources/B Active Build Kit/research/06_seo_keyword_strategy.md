## Executive Summary

B Active (bactiveph.com) enters a Philippine market experiencing two simultaneous tailwinds: a pickleball boom that has grown registered players from 244 in 2020 to 60,000+ in 2025, and a women's activewear/athleisure market forecast for sustained double-digit CAGR through 2031. The brand's dual positioning — pickleball-specialist apparel plus general premium women's activewear — gives it two distinct keyword universes to pursue. As a new WooCommerce site with a physical store in Davao City, the optimal strategy is to own Davao local SEO immediately (low competition, high relevance), build mid-tail pickleball-apparel pages over the next 3–6 months, and pursue broader activewear head terms over 12–24 months as domain authority grows.

## 1. Keyword Landscape

### 1.1 Global & PH Pickleball Apparel SEO Trend

Search interest in "pickleball" grew 42% year-over-year as of mid-2025, reaching 8.4 million global monthly searches (Glimpse). The keyword **"pickleball dress"** is the dominant apparel term globally, maintaining ~4,400 monthly searches and posting a month-over-month growth rate of ~3% (Treendly / SaleHoo). The term peaked at a normalized score of 87 in August 2025 and 82 in November 2025, suggesting tournament and seasonal purchase cycles. In the Philippines, pickleball's growth trajectory makes PH-specific pickleball apparel keywords an early-mover opportunity — local competitors are primarily paddle/equipment focused (PickleballPH, Palakol, Decathlon PH), and **no dominant Philippine brand owns the pickleball apparel SERP** (as of this research).

Skorts as a category are surging globally: searches peaked at a normalized score of 83 in June 2025 and are driven by athleisure crossover demand; "pleated tennis skorts with pockets" saw sales grow 177% from February to July 2025 (Accio/SaleHoo data).

### 1.2 Philippine Activewear Search Landscape

The Philippines activewear market is expanding rapidly with e-commerce as a primary channel. Lazada Philippines has active tag pages for **"women tennis dress"**, **"tennis skirt for women Philippines"**, **"pickleball clothing"**, and **"pickleball attire for ladies"** — confirming real on-platform search volume. Shopee PH lists "Women Pickleball Outfit 2-in-1 Training Dress" products, signalling that Shopee buyers are already using the term. ZALORA PH published a style editorial ("7 Days, 7 Pickleball Looks") — confirming editorial/commercial interest. Bench Online launched a dedicated pickleball collection, meaning even mass-market local brands see the opportunity.

## 2. Local SEO — Davao City

### 2.1 Why Local SEO Matters Immediately

Davao City has 4+ established pickleball venues (Arena Athletics with 11 courts, Paddle Up, Matina Town Square, Supersmasher Pickleball Center) and an active player community. B Active's physical store is the only known pickleball-specialist apparel retail location in the city — this is a significant early-mover advantage in local search. Local queries ("activewear store Davao", "pickleball gear Davao") have near-zero competition and very high conversion intent.

### 2.2 Google Business Profile Setup

**Primary Category:** `Sporting Goods Store`
**Secondary Categories (add up to 9 total):**
- Women's Clothing Store
- Sports Apparel Store
- Athletic Wear Retailer
- Clothing Store

**Key Attributes to Enable:**
- Women-led business
- Accepts credit cards / GCash / Maya (payment signals)
- Wheelchair accessible (if applicable)
- In-store shopping
- In-store pickup
- Online delivery / shipping available nationwide
- Identifies as women's business

**Profile Optimization Checklist:**
- [ ] Verify via video (most reliable in PH — show storefront exterior with visible address, interior, and business document)
- [ ] Upload 10+ high-quality photos: storefront, interior, product displays, team, lifestyle shots of women wearing the apparel on pickleball courts
- [ ] Write a keyword-rich business description (250 words) including: "pickleball apparel," "women's activewear," "Davao City," "pickleball dress Philippines," "sports bra," "skort," and "ships nationwide"
- [ ] Add store hours precisely; update for holidays
- [ ] Add website URL and phone number
- [ ] Enable Q&A — pre-populate with 5–8 common questions ("Do you ship nationwide?" "Do you carry pickleball paddles?" etc.)

**GBP Post Strategy:**
Post types to rotate:
1. **What's New** — new product arrivals (weekly)
2. **Event** — local Davao pickleball tournaments, B Active participation

### 3.3 Heading Structure

**Homepage H1:** `Premium Women's Activewear Made for Pickleball`
**Category page H1:** `[Category Name] for Women in the Philippines` (include keyword naturally)
**Product page H1:** Product name exactly (becomes the schema `name` property)
**Blog post H1:** Full-length title with primary keyword near the front

Use H2s for section breaks, H3s for sub-sections. Do not skip heading levels. Each page should have exactly one H1.

### 3.4 Internal Linking Strategy

1. **Breadcrumbs** on all category and product pages (Rank Math / Yoast handles this automatically with Blocksy — enable it)
2. **Collection page interlinking**: Each collection page links to 2–3 related collection pages in a "You Might Also Like" section
3. **Blog-to-collection links**: Every blog post should link to at least one relevant collection page with keyword-rich anchor text
4. **Homepage → all main collections**: Feature blocks on the homepage pass link equity to key category pages
5. **Pickleball paddle page → pickleball apparel collections**: Cross-sell links reinforce topical authority
6. **"Complete your look" module** on product pages: links to complementary products (dress → sports bra → paddle)

### 3.5 WooCommerce Product Schema

WooCommerce with Rank Math (preferred) or Yoast WooCommerce SEO generates Product schema automatically. Ensure these fields are populated for every product:

- `name` → exact product name
- `description` → 150–300 word unique description (do not copy from supplier)
- `image` → minimum 3 high-res images per product (1200px+)
- `brand` → "B Active"
- `sku` → unique SKU per variant
- `price` / `priceCurrency` → PHP
- `availability` → `InStock` or `OutOfStock` (keep current)
- `aggregateRating` → enable reviews; even 5–10 reviews trigger star display in SERPs
- `material` → fabric type (improves rich snippet eligibility)

Use Google's Rich Results Test tool after launch to validate schema output.

### 3.6 Collection Page Copy

Each collection page needs a minimum of 200–300 words of unique, keyword-rich copy — either above the product grid (short intro paragraph) or below it (longer descriptive section). Google cannot rank thin category pages. Include:
- Primary keyword in first sentence
- Secondary keywords naturally distributed
- 1–2 internal links to related categories or blog posts
- A brief brand/product benefit statement

1. **SEO**: Capture informational queries that are pre-purchase signals
2. **Topical Authority**: Signal to Google that bactiveph.com is the definitive resource for pickleball apparel in the Philippines

- **Pillar A**: Pickleball in the Philippines (sport-side authority)

Each pillar has cluster content (blog posts) that link back to the pillar page and to relevant collection pages.

## 5. Competitive / SERP Observations

### 5.1 Pickleball Apparel Philippines SERP

There is **no dominant Philippine brand** ranking for pickleball apparel keywords as of this research. The SERP for "pickleball dress Philippines" is likely populated by:
- Lazada and Shopee product listing pages (strong domain authority)
- Decathlon PH (category page)
- International brands (global domain authority)
- PickleballPH (primarily paddle-focused, not apparel-dominant)
- Bench Online (entered pickleball space with a collection)

**Implication**: B Active has a narrow but real window to become the first native PH brand ranking for pickleball apparel queries. Acting now (mid-2026) means fewer entrenched competitors than will exist in 12–18 months.

### 5.2 General Activewear Philippines SERP

This space is dominated by:
- International brands: Nike, Adidas, Under Armour
- Filipino brands with established authority: Sassa, Recess (celebrity-backed), MillennX, Lotus, Andi Activewear
- Marketplaces: Lazada, Shopee (impossible to outrank for head terms directly)
- Fashion editorial domains: Preview.ph, Spot.ph, Mega-Asia, PrettyMe.ph, Hypebae

**Implication**: B Active cannot compete for generic "activewear Philippines" in the short term. However, it *can* rank in editorial results if pitched to lifestyle/fashion editors (Pretty Me PH, Hypebae, etc.) for coverage — unlinked mentions become citations; linked mentions build domain authority.

### 5.3 Competitor Gap Analysis

| Competitor | Strengths | Gaps B Active Can Exploit |
|---|---|---|
| PickleballPH | Strong pickleball brand, equipment authority | No premium women's apparel focus; no local Davao presence |
| Bench Online | Mass market reach, brand recognition | Not pickleball-specialist; not premium |
| Decathlon PH | Pricing, breadth | Not specialist; not local Davao |
| Sassa/MillennX/Recess | Activewear credibility | Not pickleball-focused; no Davao local presence |
| Lazada/Shopee | SEO powerhouses via marketplace authority | No brand story, no specialist positioning |
