# B Active checkout checklist

**Training draft — distribute after manager release approval**

## Every customer

1. **Check the items** — Correct product, size, color and quantity.
2. **Review the total** — Tap **Review sale & payment** and read the final amount.
3. **Collect once** — Cash: count it, enter amount, confirm. Digital: customer scans with phone camera and pays on the secure page.
4. **Wait for Payment received** — Keep the goods until this cashier screen confirms payment.
5. **Write the invoice** — Issue a registered handwritten invoice for every sale and enter its number.
6. **Hand over** — Return change, give invoice and goods, tap **Confirm invoice & hand over goods**.
7. **Finish** — Check **Sale complete**, then **Start next sale**.

## Stop and ask a manager

Payment pending, payment unclear, connection lost, missing stock, wrong total, duplicate-payment concern, refund or exchange.

**Keep the same sale open. Quote its order number. Do not collect again.**

## Do

Use your own account. Check variations. Count cash. Wait for confirmed payment. Issue the handwritten invoice. Sign out when finished.

## Do not

Accept payment screenshots. Hand over while pending. Change a digital sale to cash. Make a replacement sale after an error. Use a separate QR/payment link. Change inventory or prices yourself.

## Optional email

**Submitted to email service** does not mean delivered. Use **Resend email confirmation** if needed. The email is a payment confirmation, not the handwritten tax invoice.
