# B Active manager checkout checklist

## Before opening the register

- Open the cashier page and check that new sales are enabled for the shift.
- Confirm staff have individual Sales Associate accounts, internet access, change and the registered handwritten invoice book.
- Stock counts have been verified by the owner. Ask a manager to resolve any later stock mismatch; associates must not change stock themselves.
- Check that digital payments show up in both PayMongo and the matching store order. Report any payment that stays pending.
- Train staff on the guide and show them pending, review and confirmed-payment states.

## At the end of each shift

- Open B Active cashier → Setup & daily reconciliation. The table lists the latest 100 orders; it is not a full cash-drawer ledger. Use WooCommerce for older records.
- Resolve pending/review orders using order and provider evidence. Never release stock or collect replacement payment while a digital attempt can still settle.
- Match orders to handwritten invoices, cash received/change and digital transactions.
- Count cash and reconcile opening float, payouts and other movements separately.
- Reconcile gross digital sales separately from provider fees and payouts.
- Review missing invoices, email failures and stock exceptions. Record sanitized order references and corrective actions.

## If checkout must be paused

Uncheck Enable new in-store sales and save. Verify new sales are blocked. Keep the cashier plugin, PayMongo plugin and workers active so outstanding sales can resolve. Do not delete claims, orders or reservations to clear an error.
