It started with a love for the game.

B Active was born in Davao when our founder couldn’t find pickleball wear that was beautiful, well-made, well-priced — and actually cut for her. So she made it.

Our story

Designed for an Asian fit.

Most activewear is cut for a Western frame and “adjusted” for the rest of us. We start from our own measurements, so straps sit right, lengths land where they should, and nothing digs or gapes. True to size, S–XL.
